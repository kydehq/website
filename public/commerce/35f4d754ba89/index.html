What we learned in the lab

Why stores lose agent sales.

Agents buy by listed price.

They compare every store they find and buy the cheapest match.

A benefit that isn't in the price doesn't exist.

Club perks and checkout discounts are never seen. Agents don't open a store page to look.

Design doesn't count.

Agents read product data: size, material, stock, delivery.

Bot protection can cost you buyers.

Real agents for real people get turned away without a sound.
