02 · The problem

People feel a brand. Agents read data.

People love design, pictures and stories. An agent does not care. It only reads facts: what it is, which size, which price.

What a person sees

  • Design and brand
  • Campaign and journal
  • The story of the collection
  • Your offers and drops

What an agent reads

  • What it is, size, material
  • Price
  • In stock? When does it arrive?
  • No design. No story.

The agent does not look for A Kind of Guise. It looks for “navy overshirt”. It checks the price in every store and buys the cheapest one. Even if that is your shirt in another shop. It does not visit your website to look for a better deal. We tested this.
