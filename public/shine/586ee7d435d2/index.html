The problem

Real buyers mean it. Many bots don't.

Bots fill carts with no intention of paying. Your one-off sits in a cart that will never check out.

The customer who wanted it leaves empty-handed.

The solution

Every agent, on one intent scale.

Shine tells you, for every AI agent in your shop, how ready it is to buy. Three levels, plus a maximum. You decide what each level gets.

  1. Level 1

    Browsing

    Looking around. No sign of a purchase yet.

    Browse and see prices. Nothing is held.
  2. Level 2

    Interested

    A real agent for a real person, shopping with purpose.

    Cart, wish list, back-in-stock alerts.
  3. Level 3

    Ready to buy

    It was sent to buy this, and it behaves like a buyer.

    Holds scarce pieces for 10 minutes.
  4. Maximum

    Committed

    A card hold of 10 to 20 % of the price, approved by its person. Credited when it buys, released when it does not.

    Holds and goes first.

How we know

We read the signals every agent leaves behind.

Identity, behavior, the order it carries. Our own models map these signals into a high-dimensional pattern space and place each agent on the scale, in milliseconds.

How it works

One link under every scarce product.

  1. Link for agents

    Under each scarce product: "Agents: verify to reserve."

  2. Sign in with Shine

    The agent signs in under your shop's name. Its person approves.

  3. Placed on the scale

    Browsing, interested or ready to buy. In milliseconds.

  4. Maximum: card hold

    For the rarest pieces, its person approves a hold of 10 to 20 %. Released if it does not buy.
