How it works

One purchase, start to finish.

A person tells their agent: "Buy me a blazer, up to €150, by Friday." Here is what happens.

  1. The agent

    The order is recorded.

    The agent runs Kyde. Kyde records the order at the start, before the agent reads a single web page. Nobody can change it later.

  2. Your shop

    The agent shows its Shine ID.

    Shine checks it on the spot: is this really the agent it claims to be, and does it belong to a person?

  3. Shine

    Cart against order.

    Blazer, €139, delivered Thursday. It matches what the person asked for. Shine answers: ready to buy.

  4. Your shop

    You react, your way.

    Hold the piece for 10 minutes, show a special deal, let it go first. Your rules.

  5. Shine

    The purchase gets a receipt.

    Signed: which agent, which order, what it bought. You keep it for any dispute.
