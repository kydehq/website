The intent scale

Every agent, on one intent scale.

Shine evaluates every shopping agent and tells you how close it is to buying. Three levels, plus a maximum.

We give you the signal. You do what you do best.

  1. Level 1

    Browsing

    Looking around. No sign of a purchase yet.

    For exampleYour shop, as always.
  2. Level 2

    Interested

    A real agent for a real person, shopping with purpose.

    For exampleNew arrivals and what others bought.
  3. Level 3

    Ready to buy

    It runs Kyde. We know what its person asked for, and the cart matches.

    For exampleA special deal. A piece held for 10 minutes.
  4. Maximum

    Committed

    A card hold of 10 to 20 % of the price, approved by its person. Credited when it buys, released when it does not.

    For exampleEarly access to drops and your best price.

How it works

Sits quietly in front of your cart and checkout.

  1. Connect

    You add Shine in front of your cart and checkout. Agents install nothing.

  2. Read

    Shine reads what every agent brings: its signature, its behavior and, with Kyde on board, its order.

  3. Signal

    Browsing, interested, ready to buy. You decide what each level sees.

  4. Prove

    Every agent purchase comes with a signed receipt.
