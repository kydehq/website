The problem

Real buyers mean it. Many bots don't.

Bots fill carts with no intention of paying. Your one-off sits in a cart that will never check out.

The customer who wanted it leaves empty-handed.

The solution

We don't just check who the agent is. We check that it has a real order.

Anyone can browse, as always. To hold scarce stock, an agent has to show proof. The stronger the proof, the more it may do.

Level 1

Verified agent

It is who it says it is, checked fresh, and it belongs to a person.

Can use the cart. Scarce items are not held.
Level 2

Verified task

Its person gave it a real order to buy, and what it does matches that order.

Can reserve scarce items for a few minutes.
Level 3

Deposit

A deposit, for example €10, credited when it buys. The strongest intent.

Reserves and goes first.
Level 2, way A

Agents that run Kyde. Companies running their own agents: Kyde checks the task and the behavior directly.

Level 2, way B

Big agents like ChatGPT or Gemini. They need an order signed by their person. What the agent does on your site helps you prioritise.

How it works

One link under every scarce product.

  1. Link for agents

    Under each scarce product: "Agents: verify to reserve."

  2. Sign in with Shine

    The agent signs in under your shop's name. Its person approves.

  3. Checked

    Shine checks the agent, here and now, and its order to buy.

  4. Deposit, then in

    It places €10 through your payment provider and is cleared automatically.
